Help: instrument controls & accessibility

Drag the central ring to move the instrument. Drag the S handle to rotate the stationary arm and the M handle to rotate the moving arm. Neither arm snaps into place.

Use the selector and arrow buttons for touch or keyboard adjustments. Left/down rotate counterclockwise; right/up rotate clockwise, one degree at a time. Position buttons move five illustration units.

With “Keyboard instrument control” focused: arrows move the fulcrum; Shift + arrows rotate the stationary arm; Ctrl + arrows rotate the moving arm.
